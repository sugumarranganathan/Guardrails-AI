
## Where is Context Used?

1. RAG-based chatbots
2. AI agents
3. Customer support chatbots
4. Multi-agent applications
5. Conversation memory
6. Document processing

## Practical Example

Suppose you develop a Tamil-speaking real estate chatbot.

- **Context:** Property details and customer conversation history.
- **Prompt:** Answer the customer's question in Tamil.
- **LLM:** Gemini or GPT.
- **Output:** Relevant answer in Tamil.

### Remember

Context helps AI understand what is happening; prompts tell AI what to do.
