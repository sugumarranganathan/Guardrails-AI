
# Guardrails AI – Meaning of the Name

## 1. Guardrails

Guardrails means safety barriers or protective boundaries that prevent something from going in the wrong direction.

**Examples:**
- Road guardrails prevent vehicles from falling off a bridge.
- Safety guardrails prevent people from entering dangerous areas.

## 2. AI (Artificial Intelligence)

AI is technology that enables computers to understand information, learn patterns, generate answers and perform intelligent tasks.

## 3. Guardrails AI

In Artificial Intelligence, Guardrails means setting safety rules, restrictions and validation checks to ensure AI behaves as expected.

**Simple Example:**

Imagine a road:
- Road = AI application
- Vehicle = LLM
- Guardrails = Safety rules
- Destination = Correct answer

Without guardrails, an AI application may generate harmful, incorrect or irrelevant responses.

With guardrails, we can control and validate its behaviour.

## Remember

Guardrails AI = Safety boundaries and rules for AI systems.

It helps make AI applications safer, more reliable and trustworthy.
